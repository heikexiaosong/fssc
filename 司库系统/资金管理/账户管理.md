### 【定时器】久悬户更新
- cn.ztessc.service.task.JobCenter#updateAccountSalesMark

ACCOUNT_REMIND：久悬户天数设置

FMS_ACCOUNT::LATEST_TRADING_TIME 最新交易时间
FMS_ACCOUNT::CREATE_DATE 创建时间
到今天是否超过<久悬户天数设置>

```sql
select fa.id, fa.BANK_NUMBER , fa.LATEST_TRADING_TIME , fa.CREATE_DATE,  l.CREATE_DATE as '久悬户判定时间'
  from fms_account fa 
       left join FMS_ACCOUNT_LOW_EFFICIENCY l on l.ACCOUNT_ID  = fa.ID 
 where fa.bank_number = '431250100100407138';
```

匹配电子回单与交易流水:

流水构建6要素 transactionDetailsMap：
  流水key：bankNumber-othBankNumber-transactionType-transactionCurrencyCode-transactionAmount-groupId
  transactionDetailsMap: <key, [transactionDetails]>

流水构建五要素 fiveMap：
  流水fiveKey：bankNumber-transactionType-transactionCurrencyCode-transactionAmount-groupId
  fiveMap: <fiveKey, [transactionDetails]>

回单匹配（FmsElectronicReceiptDTO ele : eleList）：
  电子回单的六要素拼接键
  transactionType = 0（付款， 支出）:
  eleKey：payerBankAccountNum-gatherBankAccountNum-transactionType-paymentCurrency-applyAmount-groupId
  transactionType = 1（收款， 收入）:
  eleKey：gatherBankAccountNum-payerBankAccountNum-transactionType-paymentCurrency-applyAmount-groupId
